Privacy Policy

Your privacy matters to us. Learn how NIRMANX collects, uses, stores and protects your information.

Last Updated: November 16, 2026

01. Introduction

Welcome to NIRMANX. NIRMANX is a construction management application designed to help construction teams organize projects, workers, expenses, payments, contracts and other business activities.

This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, stored and shared when you use our application.

By using NIRMANX, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

02. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, NIRMANX may process the following information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address and account details.
  • Project Information: Project names, descriptions, locations and progress details.
  • Worker Information: Worker names, contact details and work-related records entered by users.
  • Financial Information: Expenses, payments, budgets, bills and other project-related financial records.
  • Documents: Contracts, invoices and other files uploaded or created by users.
  • Preferences: Language, theme and currency settings.
  • Technical Information: Device and diagnostic information, if collected by the app or its service providers.

We only intend to collect information necessary for providing and improving the application's features.

03. How We Use Information

Information may be used to:

  • Create and manage user accounts.
  • Manage construction projects and teams.
  • Maintain worker and payment records.
  • Organize expenses, bills and contracts.
  • Provide application functionality and settings.
  • Maintain application security.
  • Fix technical issues and improve performance.
  • Respond to user support requests.

04. Data Storage and Security

Information is stored using the storage systems and backend services configured for NIRMANX.

We take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no digital storage or internet transmission system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

05. Sharing of Information

We do not sell users' personal information.

Information may be shared only where necessary:

  • With authorized members of a project or team, according to their access permissions.
  • With service providers that help operate the application.
  • When required by applicable law or a lawful request.
  • When necessary to protect the security and rights of users.

06. Third-Party Services

NIRMANX may use third-party services for authentication, cloud storage, databases, notifications or other application functions.

These services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

The applicable service providers and their privacy policies should be identified here before publication.

07. Data Retention

Information is retained for as long as necessary to provide the application's services, maintain records and meet applicable legal obligations.

When information is no longer required, we will take appropriate steps to delete or anonymize it, subject to applicable legal requirements.

08. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, users may have the right to:

  • Request access to their personal information.
  • Request correction of inaccurate information.
  • Request deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Ask questions about how their information is processed.
  • Withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

Contact us using the details below to make a privacy request.

09. Children's Privacy

NIRMANX is intended for construction and business management purposes and is not designed specifically for children.

We do not knowingly collect children's personal information in violation of applicable laws.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when application features, services or legal requirements change.

Updated versions will be published on this page with a revised update date.
